# Account-scoped selection and scoring

The verified session is the source of account identity. After cutover, selection list and detail require the opportunity's native account to agree with the sealed owner. Source and purchase-price evidence must match the original linked records. Preserve IDs, evidence and historical decisions; count and page with the same filter. Recommended, candidate and completed views retain their existing quality gates. Candidates with a confirmed product do not become recommendations until those gates pass.

An opportunity decision preserves the original human actor. A replay cannot return a conflicting decision or verification task. A scoring-rule draft or action replay rechecks the cached result's exact identity and live ownership before returning it. Only the verified primary account may operate its rule lifecycle; activation and rollback reject overlapping or inconsistent active ownership with HTTP 409 before updating any rule. Rescoring queues eligible owned opportunities and does not rewrite historical score runs or change thresholds. Preview is read-only.

In native account storage the scoring and profit workers lock the rule and opportunity using their original IDs and the sealed account. They reject a mismatched rule or opportunity before writing a result. New calculation results, evidence links and completion receipts retain their original input IDs and inherit the verified owner; profit requeues evaluation only for the same native opportunity and ledger owner. Pipeline completion checks the original intelligence, competitor and supplier evidence under that account. These checks do not alter qualification thresholds or historical approval actors.

Evidence refresh pins the original job, opportunity and topic under the same account and rechecks the lease before completion. Existing evidence links are not replaced: they must still point to the original owned signal and raw evidence. New links, audit and delivery share the verified account and transaction; a failed outbox write or expired lease rolls back refresh writes. Manual opportunities may finish with no evidence only after validating their original job and owner. Monitoring-rule candidacy checks the owned opportunity, rule and match and does not replace a versioned score.

Automatic evaluation admits and recovers only the same-account opportunity and its original evidence. It rechecks the native account and active attempt before renewing a lease. Active score and cost rules must match both the stored account and sealed owner before use, and their identity is checked again at queue insertion. Existing results, human-confirmed inputs and thresholds are not overwritten by this migration. Use the tests in `accountAutomaticSelectionRuntime` to verify native rules, evidence and recovery without relaunching historical jobs.

This slice adds no environment setting. It activates with the verified account runtime, not by turning on the historical rehearsal endpoint. Manual opportunity creation, member options, batch actions, and the legacy manual scoring routes are not account write surfaces in this slice. For deployment use the approved account cutover coordinator; do not independently restart the service into account mode.

That inventory is not post-cutover acceptance.
